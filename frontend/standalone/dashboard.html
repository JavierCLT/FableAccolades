BBIBest Broker Index
Sources, methodology and disclosures

Scores combine current objective facts, customer-voice evidence and attributable expert assessments across 16 dimensions. Persona weights determine the overall fit score. Confidence and evidence counts indicate coverage; facts outside their verification window are excluded. App-store and Trustpilot ratings are contextual and are not treated as a representative customer census.

Independent research from public data. Scores are estimates, not financial advice. Verify current terms with the broker before moving money.
